Disneyland Resort in Anaheim, California is the ultimate, original Disney destination and the only theme park designed and built under the direct supervision of Walt Disney. The resort consists of two neighboring theme parks—the historic Disneyland Park and Disney California Adventure Park—separated by the open-air shopping and dining district known as Downtown Disney. Current Highlights & Seasonal Events • Halloween Time: Running through October 31, the park features the iconic Giant Mickey Pumpkin, the "Halloween Screams" projection show, and the seasonal Nightmare Before Christmas overlay on the Haunted Mansion. • Oogie Boogie Bash: This separate-ticket Disney Halloween party takes place on select nights over at Disney California Adventure. • Upcoming Holiday Magic: Starting November 13, 2026, the resort transitions into the holiday season, bringing back "A Christmas Fantasy Parade" and transforming Sleeping Beauty's Castle into a winter landscape adorned with over 80,000 lights. • Tiana's Bayou Adventure: The newly reimagined log flume ride is fully operational in Bayou Country, delivering a fast-paced splash experience. Ticket Pricing & Logistics Disneyland utilizes a demand-based, multi-tier pricing structure for its single-day tickets. Prices vary heavily based on how busy the dates are projected to be: Ticket Type Price Range (Ages 10+) Details Tier 0 (Slowest Days) $104 Price floor held since 2019; typically available on select off-peak weekdays. Tiers 1–4 (Mid-Demand) $134 – $189 Prices in these intermediate tiers saw a standard $5 increase in October 2026.
